Quality & traceability

One standard, documented.

Every batch is tested in-house against EU and US residue limits — whichever is stricter — before it is cleared for packing. The certificate travels with the carton. Full HACCP protocol from intake to reefer.

  • HACCP-certified processing, end to end
  • In-house residue laboratory, every batch screened
  • EU-approved facility standards · US FDA aligned
  • BAP-aligned farm sourcing on the pond side
  • Certificate of analysis with every consignment
